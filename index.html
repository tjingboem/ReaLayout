Drag an element to move it (drag it into a neighbouring panel to move it there too — nothing reparents, it's all one shared space). Shift+click to add/remove elements from a multi-selection, then drag any of them to move the whole group together (resizing stays single-element only). Drag the small green handle to resize. Click to select, double-click the label to rename. Alt+click cycles down through anything stacked underneath if two elements overlap. Ctrl+click and hold shows a pink crosshair that follows the cursor with its x,y in plugin pixels; release to hide it. Elements whose state is "*" show in every state of their panel, so move them once and they move everywhere.
